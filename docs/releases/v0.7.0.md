## Producer Studio 0.7.0 for Windows

**Download:** `Producer-Studio-Setup-0.7.0.exe` below (about 850 MB). Per-user install, no admin rights needed. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn on first run: choose **More info → Run anyway**. Installing over an earlier version keeps your projects, settings and the VoiceStudio engine.

The app doesn't tell you about new versions yet (that waits for signed builds), so if you have 0.6.1, download and install this one yourself.

### What's new since 0.6.1
A large update: everything added to producerstudio.app since October is in the desktop app now.

**Videos from an idea**
- Make a video from an idea or a pasted script, with no recording: your own clips and images, free stock photos and clips (Wikimedia Commons, Openverse), animation scenes, and scenes the AI draws in code: 2D, Lottie motion graphics, Open Peeps people, 3D with three.js, and Blender (on this computer or a rented RunPod card).
- AI pictures and video clips with your own keys: Google Gemini, and Wan on fal.ai. A "full motion" choice makes every scene an AI clip. The same characters, at the right size next to each other, in every clip.
- Free music composed on the video's scene changes, and sound effects on the cuts.
- Videos without narration, without words on screen, or without title cards.
- Drawn scenes are more reliable: a scene that hangs is replaced instead of stopping the video, a stopped video keeps its finished drawings, and the time and cost are shown before you start.

**Editor and exports**
- Transitions on every cut at once, number boxes with steppers, brand colours in colour fields, caption box styling, shapes, full screen, and a project's own media list.
- Exports render from your clips in the colours the editor shows, cut to the parts used. Long exports check the disk first, and say which clip failed and why. Videos made of many AI clips export again: they used to stop at 18%.
- "Remove…" for clips that aren't on the timeline works again (its menu opened off the screen).

**Desktop app**
- The first start after installing works (splash screen, engine wait, tool check).
- The uninstaller asks whether to remove the VoiceStudio engine and your data (both default to No).
- Keep a project available offline; linking to the cloud shows what it will merge first.
- Optional local crash reports, and Copy diagnostics in Help.
- AI keys shared between spaces you own.
