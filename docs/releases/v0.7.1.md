## Producer Studio 0.7.1 for Windows

**Download:** `Producer-Studio-Setup-0.7.1.exe` below (about 850 MB). Per-user install, no admin rights needed. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn on first run: choose **More info → Run anyway**. Installing over 0.7.0 or earlier keeps your projects, settings and the VoiceStudio engine.

The app doesn't tell you about new versions yet (that waits for signed builds), so if you have 0.7.0, download and install this one yourself.

### What's new since 0.7.0

**A music library**
- 20 free music tracks in the editor's **Audio** tab, under Music library. They range from friendly explainer mallets to lo-fi, cinematic, synthwave and trap, each with its style, mood and length.
- Listen to any track before you use it, then **+** puts it on the timeline at the playhead.
- The tracks are made by the app itself: no key, no internet, free to use in your videos. The first time you listen to a track, the app composes it on this computer, which can take up to a minute. After that it plays straight away.
- Your own sounds in the Audio panel can be heard before you add them too.

**Hear the brief's sound before you pick it**
- In Producer's brief, **Listen** plays a track in the chosen music style, and **Listen to the effects** plays the sound effects that go with it.

**Captions your way**
- In the brief, keep or turn off words on screen. If you keep them, choose how they look (Clean, Boxed, Karaoke, Word pop or Editorial), the font (the brand's or one of 12), the size (small, medium or large) and the place (top, middle or bottom), with a sample that shows the result before the video is made.
