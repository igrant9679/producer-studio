## Producer Studio 0.7.2 for Windows

**Download:** `Producer-Studio-Setup-0.7.2.exe` below (about 850 MB). Per-user install, no admin rights needed. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn on first run: choose **More info → Run anyway**. Installing over 0.7.1 or earlier keeps your projects, settings and the VoiceStudio engine.

The app doesn't tell you about new versions yet (that waits for signed builds), so if you have 0.7.1, download and install this one yourself.

### What's new since 0.7.1

**Music that plays in tune**
- The music Producer composes for a video, and the brief's **Listen** samples, were out of tune: several copies of each note sounded slightly apart and beat against each other, and the bell, glass, kalimba, glockenspiel and marimba sounds had overtones between the notes. Each instrument now plays within a few cents of its notes. Music composed for a video from now on uses the retuned instruments; music already in a video stays as it was until the video is assembled again.

**The music library moves to producerstudio.app**
- The 20 composed tracks in the editor's Audio tab are replaced, on producerstudio.app, by a media library of 20 songs made with Google's Lyria, 10 animations and 10 full-motion clips, to watch or hear first and add to a video. That library isn't in the desktop app yet: the Audio and Media tabs say so. Producer's brief still offers music composed for your video here, in all its styles.

**Your brand on your videos**
- **Your logo** in Producer's brief stamps your brand kit's logo (or its name, until you add a logo) small in the bottom-right corner through the scenes. A kit's logo also goes above the title and on the closing card.
- **Brand the cover** in Export draws the cover frame with your title in your brand's headline font over an accent bar, and your logo in the corner. A video Producer made starts with it ticked. Exports & sharing shows each export's own cover, with a **Thumbnail** button to download it.

**Better videos from Producer**
- Producer checks each stock photo and clip with your AI before using it, and passes over one that doesn't show what the scene is about (one small AI call per picture looked at). Without an AI it uses the first one, as before.
- A title on two lines no longer covers the name under it on the title card.
- On animated-text scenes, each point appears beside its dot instead of sliding across it.
- The brief now uses your brand kit for a new video too (before, it only did once the video had been made).

**In the editor**
- **Watch** a clip in the Media tab with sound before putting it on the timeline.
- The editor's Help has a page on the media library.
