## Producer Studio 0.7.3 for Windows

**Download:** `Producer-Studio-Setup-0.7.3.exe` below (about 850 MB). Per-user install, no admin rights needed. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn on first run: choose **More info → Run anyway**. Installing over 0.7.2 or earlier keeps your projects, settings and the VoiceStudio engine.

The app doesn't tell you about new versions yet (that waits for signed builds), so if you have 0.7.2, download and install this one yourself.

### What's new since 0.7.2

**The media library in the desktop app**
- Link this computer to your producerstudio.app account (**Settings → This computer**) and the media library appears in the editor: 20 songs in the **Audio** tab and 10 animations and 10 full-motion clips under **Media → Clip library**, to hear or watch first and add to a video.
- Each song or clip is fetched from producerstudio.app the first time you play or add it, then kept on this computer, so it plays and adds without the internet afterwards.
- Producer uses it here too: a new brief's music is the library song that suits its tone, scenes can show library clips, and **Use as this video's music** on a song swaps a video's music for it, keeping the version before in Versions.
- Not linked, the panels say how to get the library. Remaking a library item for your topic stays on producerstudio.app.

**Stock pictures that fit, and what was passed over**
- When Producer's AI passes over stock photos or clips that don't show what a scene is about, the script now says what they showed, under the one it used.
- When a stock search finds nothing, Producer tries a shorter one ("baker selling bread market stall", then "baker selling bread"…), and the AI still checks each picture against the whole scene.

**Behind the scenes**
- The installer build unzips its speech-to-text tool more reliably, and stops if it is missing rather than shipping without it.
